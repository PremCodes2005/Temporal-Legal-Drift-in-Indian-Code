# Phase 6 Controlled Scenario Protocol

**Status:** Evidence-linked authoring scaffolds implemented; compliance scenarios and expert gold not authored.

Scaffolds are created only for internally corroborated amendment/date records present in the Phase 5 workload. They prefill stable identifiers, source links and pre/post reference dates. They deliberately leave facts, legal questions, pre-amendment governing versions, expected answers, compliance consequences, expected-change labels, categories and expert rationales unresolved.

No model output may populate a gold field. A future scenario pair must hold facts constant, cite an approved applicability determination, cover a registered scenario category, keep materiality separate from consequence and retain independent review/adjudication history.
